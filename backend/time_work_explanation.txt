QUESTION:
A and B together can do a certain amount of work in $x$ days. Working alone, A and B can do the same work in $(x + 8)$ and $(x + 18)$ days, respectively . A and B together will complete $\frac{5}{6}$ of the same work in:

================================================================================
AI EXPLANATION:
================================================================================

**1. Extreme Shortcut Solution** 
* $x = \sqrt{8 \times 18} = \sqrt{144} = 12$ days
* A and B's 1 day work: $\frac{1}{x} = \frac{1}{12}$
* $\frac{5}{6}$ work $\rightarrow \frac{5}{6} \times 12 = 10$ days

**2. Ranker's Hack** 
Use the pattern: $x = \sqrt{ab}$, then apply the fraction of work to find the number of days.
